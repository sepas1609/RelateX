## Reporting a Vulnerability

Security of database schemas and credentials is top priority.

If you discover a security vulnerability in RelateX:

1. **Do not create a public issue.**
2. Email your findings directly to the maintainer or submit a Private Security Advisory on GitHub.
3. Include detailed steps to reproduce the vulnerability, along with potential attack vectors and impact assessment.

We will acknowledge receipt within 48 hours and work with you on a timely resolution and release patch.
